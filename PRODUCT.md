# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Law school admissions readers, attorneys and legal mentors, recruiters, and fellow students. They arrive from a resume link or LinkedIn, skim in under two minutes, and judge credibility fast. Desktop-first (admissions/recruiters), but mobile must hold up.

## Product Purpose

Kaden Huynh's personal portfolio (kaden.me) — presents academic writing, education, work experience, leadership, and coding projects to support law school applications (UCLA / Berkeley targets) and professional networking toward corporate law.

## Brand Personality

Scholarly, precise, understated. An editorial, legal-brief feel: serif display (EB Garamond), mono metadata, restrained gold accent on near-black (dark) or white (light). Confidence through restraint — nothing loud, nothing decorative without purpose.

## Anti-references

- Generic developer-portfolio templates (hero gradients, emoji, "I build things for the web")
- SaaS landing-page grammar (metric heroes, gradient text, glassmorphism)
- Anything that reads flashy or salesy — the audience is legal/academic

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence over claims** — writing samples, GPA, real projects carry the argument; the design frames them quietly.
2. **Editorial restraint** — one accent color, generous whitespace, typographic hierarchy does the work.
3. **Every section scannable in seconds** — recruiters skim; key facts (school, GPA, dates) must be visible without interaction.
4. **Motion as polish, never spectacle** — staggered reveals, the tri-fold map; all reduced-motion safe.
5. **Content lives in `src/data/content.js`** — components never hardcode text.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG target, but maintain: reduced-motion alternatives (already via MotionConfig + CSS), keyboard-operable cards/modal, visible focus rings, ≥4.5:1 body-text contrast in both themes.
